# Ventox name and branding

Ventox is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later. This policy covers the project's name and the visual identity used by official builds, not the code.

- GPL-covered builds may be redistributed, including commercially, under the terms of the GPL.
- Modified builds must use their own name and icon and must not present themselves as the official Ventox build.
- The Ventox name and the icon and screenshots used by official builds are not licensed for use in a way that suggests endorsement by this project.
- Accurate references such as "based on Ventox" are allowed.
- This policy does not claim ownership of the upstream Vento project or its assets, which remain with their authors.
- Report misleading redistributions through the repository's issue tracker.

This is a usage policy, not a trademark registration.
